About Me

I am an Assistant Engineer at Power Grid Bangladesh PLC, working in the operation and maintenance of 132/33 kV grid substations. I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2019. My undergraduate thesis focused on T-Gate AlInN/AlN/GaN HEMT design and analysis using Silvaco ATLAS TCAD for high-power and RF applications. I am passionate about developing high-efficiency, reliable, and intelligent wide-bandgap (GaN, SiC) power electronic systems for future energy and transportation applications.
