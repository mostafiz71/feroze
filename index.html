Reading Passage:
In recent years, many cities have faced challenges related to traffic congestion. With the increasing number of vehicles on the road, commuting has become time-consuming and stressful for many people. These issues have led city planners to explore alternative transportation options to improve traffic flow. One popular solution is the expansion of public transportation systems such as buses and subways. By providing efficient and affordable transportation alternatives, cities hope to reduce the number of private vehicles on the road, thus easing congestion and improving air quality. However, investments in infrastructure and public willingness to use these systems remain important factors for success.
